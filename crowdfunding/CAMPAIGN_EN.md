# ZettaCore Nexus — crowdfunding campaign (draft)

**Status: internal draft. This is not a live campaign or an investment offer.**

## Title
Help build ZettaCore Nexus: an AI assistant designed around memory and user control

## Summary
ZettaCore Nexus is an early-stage prototype exploring continuity across conversations, user-reviewable and deletable memory, permission-based tools, and future online and local editions.

Today, we have a public web demo with simulated replies and an initial API foundation. The demo is **not connected to a real AI model** and does not yet offer accounts, cloud sync, subscriptions or payments. Funding would support the next development milestone, security testing and community feedback.

## What exists today
- A navigable web prototype with simulated chat.
- Public source repository and project documentation.
- A minimal Node.js API foundation and experimental model-provider adapters.
- A plan for an installable web app (PWA).

## What still needs to be built
- Real model integration and evaluation.
- Accounts, secure sessions and a database.
- Persistent memory with review, export and deletion controls.
- Usage quotas, abuse prevention and production security.
- Subscription billing and an admin dashboard.
- Native apps if demand justifies the cost.

## Proposed funding goal
**Working target: €5,000**, subject to a validated budget, platform eligibility, fees and tax review before launch.

Indicative allocation:
- €2,000 — development, testing and security review.
- €1,000 — infrastructure and model evaluation.
- €750 — design, website and launch assets.
- €500 — documentation and distribution preparation.
- €750 — contingency, platform fees and unexpected obligations.

This estimate does not guarantee the entire product can be completed within the target. Scope will be prioritized and updates will be shared transparently.

## Possible rewards
Final rewards depend on platform rules and our ability to deliver:
- Community supporter acknowledgement, optional.
- Beta-testing invitation when an experimental build is ready.
- Optional acknowledgement as a founding supporter.
- Early access to future features after they are built.
- A limited number of Nexus Plus months only after subscriptions and costs have been validated.

We will not offer equity, financial returns, or features that do not exist. We will not promise unlimited AI usage or a delivery date before validating the roadmap.

## Proposed milestones
1. Validate scope, requirements and budget.
2. Build secure backend, accounts and provider tests.
3. Add persistent memory and privacy controls.
4. Invite a limited beta with usage quotas.
5. Pilot subscriptions and broaden distribution if safety and unit economics support it.

Dates will be published once resources, platform approval and technical results are known.

## Risks and transparency
AI development costs can exceed estimates; providers can change prices and quotas; app stores can reject releases; and some features may take longer than expected. We will communicate scope changes and never commit secrets or user data to the public repository.

## Pre-launch checklist
- Confirm the legal entity receiving and reporting funds.
- Confirm the project owner and country meet platform eligibility.
- Review fees, taxes, delivery obligations and refund policies.
- Record an original demo video and prepare accurate screenshots.
- Validate the budget and schedule.
- Publish a privacy notice and support contact.
- Do not frame the campaign as an investment or promise returns.
