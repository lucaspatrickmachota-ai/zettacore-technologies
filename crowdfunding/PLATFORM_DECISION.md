# Crowdfunding platform decision — pre-launch

**No platform has been selected and no campaign has been published.**

## Evaluation criteria
Score each candidate against:
1. Country and legal-entity eligibility.
2. Who may receive the funds and how identity is verified.
3. Fixed versus flexible funding model and what happens if the target is missed.
4. Platform and payment processing fees.
5. Tax documents, VAT and accounting obligations.
6. Rules for software/AI projects and reward delivery.
7. Refunds, chargebacks, campaign cancellation and customer support.
8. Whether the project can honestly deliver the proposed rewards.
9. Public communication and reporting requirements.

## Shortlist to investigate
- Kickstarter — rewards-based campaigns; verify creator country eligibility and current rules.
- Indiegogo — technology and product campaigns; verify current campaign types, fees and eligibility.
- Goteo — consider if the project adopts a suitable open-knowledge/community model and meets its rules.
- Open Collective — consider for transparent ongoing community support, depending on fiscal host and legal structure.

## Go/no-go gates
Do not launch until:
- The legal recipient of funds is identified and eligible.
- The corporate purpose and actual project activity have been reviewed.
- A costed budget and milestone plan exist.
- Rewards have realistic costs and fulfilment capacity.
- Campaign copy distinguishes current prototype from future roadmap.
- Terms, risk disclosure, privacy contact, taxes and refunds are reviewed.
- A plan exists for transparent progress reports and unused/insufficient funds.

## Legal and entity caution
The owner/recipient should not be chosen casually. ZettaCore Technologies is a brand/project; confirm which legal entity, if any, will contract with supporters. Review the entity's registered activity, country, tax obligations and platform rules with a qualified adviser before taking money.
