# Nexus worldwide distribution checklist

## Stage A — public web/PWA
- [ ] Confirm HTTPS and the latest Render deployment is live.
- [ ] Validate web manifest and service worker on desktop and mobile.
- [ ] Test installation instructions for Android Chrome and iOS Safari Add to Home Screen.
- [ ] Test offline fallback and cache update behaviour.
- [ ] Publish a clear prototype disclaimer and privacy notice.
- [ ] Add accessible contact/support and issue-reporting links.
- [ ] Add privacy-conscious aggregate analytics only after notice and consent requirements are reviewed.

## Stage B — early community
- [ ] Create an accurate Product Hunt launch page with real screenshots.
- [ ] Prepare GitHub README, roadmap, changelog and issue templates.
- [ ] Share a useful technical build log on relevant communities; follow each community's self-promotion rules.
- [ ] Invite a small beta group and gather consent-based feedback.
- [ ] Do not buy downloads or fake reviews.

## Stage C — crowdfunding
- [ ] Confirm platform eligibility and legal beneficiary.
- [ ] Validate itemized costs, fees, tax treatment and delivery commitments.
- [ ] Record a demo that clearly distinguishes working prototype from planned features.
- [ ] Prepare reward terms and fulfilment plan.
- [ ] Add campaign risks, privacy contact and refund/consumer terms.
- [ ] Launch only after all details are reviewed.

## Stage D — native stores
- [ ] Decide if a wrapper or dedicated native app is justified.
- [ ] Prepare signing, update process, permissions and crash reporting.
- [ ] Review Google Play and Apple rules for AI apps, privacy, account deletion and digital subscriptions.
- [ ] Decide whether subscriptions are purchased on the web, in-app, or both, after reviewing each store's current billing rules.
- [ ] Budget for developer accounts, review time and ongoing maintenance.
- [ ] Submit Android and iOS builds only after security and functionality testing.

## Candidate discovery channels
- Product Hunt — launch visibility.
- GitHub — open technical progress and issue tracking.
- Reddit and Hacker News — community discussion, following each community's rules.
- Indie Hackers — founder feedback.
- Google Play and Apple App Store — native app distribution after stable releases.
- Samsung Galaxy Store and Huawei AppGallery — additional Android distribution if eligible.
- F-Droid — only if an Android build meets its free/open-source and inclusion policies.
- Direct PWA link — broad reach without a store listing, but discoverability still requires marketing and search optimization.

Listing is not a guarantee of visibility or downloads. Prioritize product quality, honest positioning, community feedback and sustainable usage costs.
